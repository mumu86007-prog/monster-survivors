10 Best Free FPS Games Online — Browser Shooters Ranked

By omggame.store editorial team · 5 min read

First-person shooters in a browser? Yes — and they're surprisingly good. From tactical CS-style gameplay to zombie survival co-op, browser FPS games have evolved dramatically. Here are the 10 best, ranked by gameplay depth, polish, and fun factor.

  1. CS Online — The most complete tactical FPS in any browser. Bomb defusal, hostage rescue, economy management, realistic recoil patterns. If you can only play one browser FPS, make it this one.
  2. Masked Special Forces — Competitive multiplayer with diverse weapons and maps. Team-based combat with solid gunplay.
  3. WarStrike — Best single-player FPS campaign in a browser. Cinematic story, destructible environments, checkpoints.
  4. ArmedForces IO — Class-based military FPS. Choose assault, sniper, medic, or engineer. Large battlefields.
  5. FPS Strike — Fast-paced arena shooter with quick respawns. Best for casual FPS sessions.
  6. CobraZ IO — Low-poly arena FPS. Smooth performance, fast matches, pure run-and-gun fun.
  7. Masked Forces Zombie Survival — Co-op zombie FPS. Team up, share ammo, survive together.
  8. Crazy Strike Force — Over-the-top FPS with ridiculous weapons. Not serious, very fun.
  9. Zombie Sniper — Sniper-focused zombie elimination. Satisfying headshots and upgrade progression.
  10. Legendary Sniper — Tactical sniper with realistic ballistics. Wind, distance, and bullet drop matter.
