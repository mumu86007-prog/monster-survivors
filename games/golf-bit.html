Why it fits a quiet break

Golf Bit reduces golf to a compact angle-and-power puzzle. Each hole gives you a clear beginning and end, with room to pause before taking a shot. It is ideal when you want a little spatial thinking and a satisfying finish without committing to a long game.

How to play

Use the mouse to aim and set the power for each shot.
