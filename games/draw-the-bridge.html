Why it fits a quiet break

Draw The Bridge is a gentle experiment game: make a line, observe what happens, and adjust it. It gives your attention a small engineering-style task without requiring fast reactions. The best way to play is to try one simple idea at a time instead of forcing a perfect solution.

How to play

Click and drag to draw a bridge that carries the vehicle to its goal.
