Why it is on the trend desk

Fall Brainrots joins the Trend Desk as a current meme-style obstacle game, not as a promise of a peaceful break. Its wobbly movement, quick restarts, and multiplayer race format are best for a small burst of playful attention. The useful expectation is simple: one run can be enough, and mistakes are part of the joke rather than something to optimise. If you want no timers or a slower task, use the quiet-pick filters instead.
