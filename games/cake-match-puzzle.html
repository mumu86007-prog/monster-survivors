Why it fits a quiet break

Cake Match Puzzle is a bright, low-commitment choice for someone who wants to sort and combine objects rather than react quickly. Its food theme and clear board make progress easy to see. Treat each level as a small task, not a score to chase, and take a break between rounds.

How to play

Use the mouse to select and combine matching cakes.
