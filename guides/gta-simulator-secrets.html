GTA Simulator: Hidden Secrets, Best Cars & Mission Walkthrough

By omggame.store editorial team · 9 min read

GTA Simulator packs an impressive amount of open-world content into a browser game. With a massive city to explore, dozens of vehicles to commandeer, and a full mission system, there's more depth here than most players realize. This guide reveals everything the game doesn't tell you.

Essential Knowledge: The police wanted system goes from 1-5 stars. At 1 star, police will chase but give up easily. At 3+ stars, helicopters and SWAT teams deploy. At 5 stars, the military gets involved. Hiding in parking garages or alleyways for 60 seconds resets your wanted level at 4 stars or below.

Fastest Cars Ranked (With Locations)

Not all vehicles are created equal. Here's what our testing revealed:

  1. Supercar (red sports car): Found in the downtown financial district parking garage, level 3. Top speed unmatched. Terrible off-road.
  2. Police Interceptor: Ironically found at the police station. Best all-rounder with good armor and respectable speed. Survives crashes that destroy sports cars.
  3. Motorcycle (black sport bike): Spawns near the beach boardwalk. Fastest acceleration in the game and can squeeze through gaps cars can't. Zero protection from gunfire.
  4. SUV (black truck): Found in suburban driveways. Slow but nearly indestructible. The best vehicle for surviving 5-star wanted levels.
  5. Sedan (white taxi): Everywhere. Nothing special, but readily available when you need any vehicle fast.

Making Money Fast

Money buys weapons, armor, and upgrades. Here's the optimal income strategy:

  • Main missions give the best payouts. Prioritize the storyline until payouts drop off around mission 15.
  • Side hustles (taxi driving, delivery jobs) pay less per minute but are risk-free. Good for grinding while avoiding police heat.
  • Rampage bonuses: Causing chaos generates wanted stars, which spawns more police vehicles to destroy, which drops ammo and cash pickups. High risk, high reward.
  • Hidden briefcases: There are 12 briefcases hidden across the map (rooftops, alleys, under bridges). Each contains $500-2000. Find them all once for a massive early-game boost.

Combat: Don't Play It Like Call of Duty

GTA Simulator's combat punishes run-and-gun tactics. Here's what works:

  • Always use cover. Press against walls and objects. You take 90% less damage behind cover.
  • Aim for the head. Headshots deal 3x damage. Against armored enemies, this is the only reliable way to kill quickly.
  • Sniper rifles are OP. Find one on rooftops in the industrial district. One-shot kills at any range. Essential for missions with large enemy counts.
  • Grenades clear rooms. Before entering buildings during missions, toss a grenade through the door. It clears 80% of the enemies inside.

Losing the Police: The Definitive Method

  1. Immediately break line of sight — turn corners, enter tunnels, drive through parking garages.
  2. Once out of sight, switch vehicles. Police track your last seen vehicle, not you.
  3. Hide in an alley or parking structure. Don't move. Wait 60 seconds.
  4. At 5 stars, this won't work — you need to reach a safehouse (green house icon on map) or survive for 5 real-time minutes (nearly impossible).
