Editorial policy

How omggame.store makes its picks

We publish practical guides for choosing low-pressure and light-focus browser games. The playable games are supplied by third-party providers; our editorial work is the selection, comparison, explanation, and update of the collection.

What we review

We look at the visible first action, basic controls, pace, level of pressure, natural stopping point, and the fit between a game’s presentation and its labels. We do not publish invented ratings, download claims, or health promises. A recommendation means that the current game loop appears relevant to the stated guide, not that it will suit every player.

Game sources

Game pages use disclosed OnlineGames.io iframe embeds. We do not claim ownership of the embedded games, their artwork, their advertising, or their future updates. Each game page links directly to its provider source. If an embed changes, fails, or no longer fits the collection, readers can contact us so we can review it.

Our update practice

Guides and game pages display their editorial update date. We revise them when a game source changes, a control description becomes inaccurate, or a guide needs clearer distinctions. We prefer a smaller maintained collection over a large automated directory.

Contact

For corrections, source concerns, or editorial feedback, email contact@omggame.store. We aim to reply within 48 hours.
