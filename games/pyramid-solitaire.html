Why it fits a quiet break

Pyramid Solitaire changes the familiar card routine into a compact matching puzzle. Finding pairs that make thirteen provides a small, satisfying objective while still leaving room to pause, look at the board, and make a decision without pressure.

How to play

Select exposed cards that total thirteen. Kings can be removed on their own.
