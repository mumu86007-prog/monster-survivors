Advertising policy

Where advertising may appear

Advertising, if enabled after approval, supports original editorial guides. It does not direct readers to click, does not affect our recommendations, and does not appear inside or beside game controls.

Eligible pages

A future display-ad placement may appear only on a substantial original guide after the reader has reached meaningful publisher content. The position will be clearly separated from headings, links, buttons, and game interactions. We will not place more advertising or paid promotion than publisher content on a page.

Ineligible pages

We do not place external Google display ads on playable game pages, the games directory, legal pages, navigation screens, empty states, or pages whose primary function is to launch a third-party iframe. Provider advertising within a third-party game is controlled by that provider, not by omggame.store.

Editorial independence and disclosure

We do not sell rankings or alter editorial labels in exchange for payment. Any sponsored material, affiliate relationship, or paid placement will be disclosed clearly on the relevant page before the associated content. Advertising will never be presented as editorial advice.

Current status

No Google display advertising is active on omggame.store at this time. This policy describes the boundary that will apply if the site is approved and advertising is later configured.
