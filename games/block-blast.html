Why it fits a quiet break

Block Blast suits a short reset because there is no falling-piece timer asking for a fast reaction. The useful decision is simply where a shape leaves room for the next one. Stop after one tidy clear or stay longer when you want a little quiet focus.

How to play

Drag a block onto the grid. Complete rows or columns to clear them.
