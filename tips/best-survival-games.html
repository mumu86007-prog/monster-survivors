10 Best Free Survival Games — Craft, Build & Survive Online

By omggame.store editorial team · 5 min read

Survival games tap into something primal — the satisfaction of starting with nothing and building your way to security. These 10 free browser survival games let you gather resources, craft tools, build shelters, and fight to survive, all without downloads.

Sandbox Survival

  • Cube Worlds — The closest browser experience to Minecraft. Mine, craft, build, survive. Multiple biomes, day/night cycle.
  • CubeCraft Survival — Multiplayer block survival with deep crafting. Build and survive with friends.
  • Survival Craft — Harder survival emphasis. Scarce resources, aggressive enemies, genuine challenge.
  • Survival Island — Tropical survival. Fish, farm, build, explore ancient ruins. Beautiful environment.

Action Survival

  • Monster Survivors — Roguelite survival against monster waves. Upgrade your character differently each run.
  • 99 Nights in the Forest — Atmospheric horror survival. 99 nights of supernatural threats. Stunning cel-shaded visuals.
  • Five Nights at Poppy — FNAF-style survival horror. Resource management, jump scares, survive until 6 AM.

Zombie Survival

  • Masked Forces Zombie Survival — Co-op FPS against zombie hordes.
  • Zombie War Defense — Tower defense survival. Build barricades, fend off the undead.
  • Zombie Sniper — Sniper survival from rooftop positions.
