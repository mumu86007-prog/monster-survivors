Why it fits a quiet break

Jungle Mart is selected for its clear stocking-and-serving rhythm and friendly animal setting. It can get more active as the store grows, so begin with a small target such as filling one shelf. The appeal is visible order and small progress, not competitive speed.

How to play

Use the mouse to stock shelves, serve customers, and manage upgrades.
