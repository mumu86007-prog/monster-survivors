Why it fits a quiet break

Cat Simulator is included for a curious, light-hearted break rather than deep challenge. It has more movement than the tile games, so it is best when you want to wander and discover small interactions. Pick it for a playful reset, not for a calm, still puzzle session.

How to play

Use the keyboard to move and the on-screen prompts to interact.
