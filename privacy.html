Privacy information

Last reviewed August 31, 2026

Information we collect directly

omggame.store does not require an account and does not ask visitors to submit names, passwords, payment details, or profiles. If you email us, your email service and our mailbox provider may process the message so that we can reply.

Third-party game embeds

Playable pages load games in iframes from OnlineGames.io. Those providers operate their own services and may process technical information, cookies, or advertising-related data under their own policies. The source link on every game page lets you open the provider version directly. Please review a provider’s policy before relying on its service.

Advertising status

omggame.store does not currently load a Google AdSense script or serve Google display ads. If advertising is enabled after site approval, this policy will be updated before activation to explain the relevant technologies, choices, and consent requirements. See our advertising policy for placement boundaries.

Your choices and contact

You can use browser settings to control cookies and site data from third-party embeds. For privacy questions about omggame.store, email contact@omggame.store.
