Fireboy and Watergirl Complete Walkthrough — All Temple Puzzles Solved

By omggame.store editorial team · 6 min read

Fireboy and Watergirl (Fire and Water) is one of the most beloved cooperative puzzle games ever made. But some temple puzzles can be genuinely tricky. This walkthrough covers the core mechanics every player needs to understand, plus solutions for the most commonly stuck-on puzzles.

Core Mechanics Every Player Must Know

  • Fireboy dies in water. Watergirl dies in fire. Both die in green toxic goo. This is the foundational rule that all puzzles build upon.
  • Gems match characters. Red gems go to Fireboy. Blue gems go to Watergirl. You can't collect the wrong color — but you don't need every gem to finish (they're for bonus score).
  • Both characters must reach their exit doors. Fireboy enters the red door. Watergirl enters the blue door. Both must arrive to complete the level.
  • One character can hold a switch while the other passes. Most puzzles require this. Stand on the switch, hold position, let your partner through.

Common Puzzle Types and Solutions

Switch & Door Puzzles

The most common type. One character stands on a pressure plate to open a door or disable a hazard. The other passes through. After passing, the second character needs to find a way to let the first character through — usually by finding another switch on their side.

Elevator Coordination

Elevators move when weight is on them. Both characters must step on simultaneously to move platforms. One player stepping off early can send the other player into spikes or water/fire. Count down: "3-2-1-step" to coordinate.

Laser Grids

Lasers activate in patterns — on-off-on-off. Watch the timing for 2-3 cycles before attempting to pass. Move during the "off" window. Don't stop mid-grid — commit and go.

Moving Platforms

These move on fixed paths. Watch the path for a full cycle before jumping on. Jump OFF before the platform reaches a hazard. The natural instinct is to ride platforms as long as possible — this kills you.

Co-op Communication Tips

  • Call your actions. "I'm stepping on the switch now." "I'm jumping to the platform." Verbal coordination prevents 90% of deaths.
  • One player leads, one follows. Decide who's the "navigator" for each puzzle. Two people trying to direct each other leads to chaos.
  • Be patient with your partner. The game is designed to create friction. The couples who laugh at mistakes have more fun than the ones who blame each other.

Solo Play Strategy

Playing solo requires controlling both characters with one hand on WASD and one on Arrow Keys. It's harder but teaches you to multitask. Focus on moving one character at a time — get them to a safe position, then switch. Don't try to move both simultaneously unless you're experienced.
