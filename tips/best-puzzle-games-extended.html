10 More Must-Play Puzzle Games — Hidden Gems & Brain Teasers

By omggame.store editorial team · 4 min read

Everyone knows 2048 and Solitaire. But the puzzle genre has so much more to offer. These 10 games are lesser-known gems that deserve just as much attention.

  • Find It — Beautifully illustrated hidden object scenes. Relaxing search gameplay.
  • Draw The Bridge — Creative physics puzzles where you draw the solution.
  • Draw Here — Guide balls to targets by drawing ramps and funnels.
  • Draw the Bird Path — Guide adorable birds home. Perfect for families.
  • Nova Craft — Element-combining discovery game. Start with fire, water, earth, air.
  • Cake Match Puzzle — Delicious match-3 with dessert theme.
  • Tile Match — Beautiful 3D tile matching with charming designs.
  • Mahjong Solitaire — Classic Chinese tile-matching with modern polish.
  • Chess Online — The ultimate strategy puzzle. Beautiful 3D board.
  • Four Colors — UNO-style card strategy. Simple rules, deep tactics.
