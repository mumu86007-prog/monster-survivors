Why it fits a quiet break

Tile Match is a better fit for a calm break than a high-score chase because its basic action is a simple visual match. It works best when you want a little attention on colours and shapes without needing keyboard speed or a competitive goal.

How to play

Use the mouse to select matching tiles and clear the board.
