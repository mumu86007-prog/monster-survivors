How to Escape Police at Any Wanted Level — GTA Simulator & Chase Games

By omggame.store editorial team · 5 min read

Getting a wanted level is easy. Losing it? That's the hard part. Whether you're playing GTA Simulator, Escape Car, or Police Chase Drifter, the principles of police evasion are the same. This guide covers proven escape tactics for every wanted level.

The Universal Escape Method (Works at 1-4 Stars)

  1. Break line of sight immediately. Turn corners, enter tunnels, drive through parking garages. The moment police can't see you, your escape begins.
  2. Switch vehicles. Police track your last seen vehicle. Ditch it in an alley, steal another car, and drive away calmly. Don't speed — that draws attention.
  3. Hide and wait. Park in an alley, parking structure, or behind a building. Turn off the engine. Wait 60 seconds. Your wanted level drops.

Wanted Level-Specific Tactics

1 Star — Minor Offense

Just drive away. Police give up quickly at 1 star. Don't bother hiding — distance alone works.

2 Stars — Active Pursuit

Police patrols actively search. Break line of sight with 2-3 quick turns. An alley or tunnel works instantly. Hide for 30 seconds.

3 Stars — Helicopter Deployed

Helicopters make outdoor hiding impossible. You need covered shelter: parking garages, tunnels, train stations. The helicopter can't see through roofs. Wait underground for 45 seconds.

4 Stars — SWAT Response

Roadblocks appear on major roads. Avoid highways entirely — use side streets and alleys. SWAT vehicles are armored — don't engage. Find underground parking and wait 60 seconds.

5 Stars — Military Response

At 5 stars, hiding doesn't work. You need to reach a safehouse (green house icon on map) or survive for 5 real-time minutes. Use an armored vehicle (SUV or Police Interceptor). Don't stop moving. Zigzag to avoid tank fire. Pray.

Best Escape Vehicles

  • Motorcycle — Fastest through alleys, can squeeze through gaps cars can't. Best for urban escapes.
  • SUV — Survives crashes and gunfire. Best for 4-5 star survival.
  • Sports Car — Fastest on highways. Best for creating distance quickly at 1-3 stars.
