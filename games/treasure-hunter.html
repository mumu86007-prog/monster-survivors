Why it fits a quiet break

Treasure Hunter is a gentle adventure choice for a player who wants a little exploration without a big campaign. It has more movement and platforming than our no-timer puzzles, so it is best treated as light focus. Stop after one discovery or a completed area rather than pushing through every level.

How to play

Use the keyboard to move and jump, and the mouse when prompted.
