Why it fits a quiet break

Fast Food Manager is a management-flavoured game for people who like upgrading and arranging a simple system. It can become busy, so it is better described as light focus than as a fully quiet game. Play one upgrade or one short shift, then step away before it turns into a rush.

How to play

Use the mouse to manage the restaurant, staff, and upgrades.
