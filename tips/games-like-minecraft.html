Games Like Minecraft — 8 Free Browser Sandbox & Building Games

By omggame.store editorial team · 5 min read

Minecraft is irreplaceable, but sometimes you want that block-building, survival-crafting experience without installing anything. These 8 free browser games capture different aspects of what makes Minecraft great — from creative building to survival crafting to creature collecting.

  • Cube Worlds — The closest overall Minecraft experience in a browser. Block worlds, mining, crafting, building, multiple biomes, day/night cycle. Play this first.
  • CubeCraft Survival — Multiplayer Minecraft alternative. Build and survive with friends. Deep crafting, enchanting, boss battles.
  • Survival Craft — Hardcore survival emphasis. Scarcer resources, tougher enemies. For players who find Minecraft too easy.
  • Survival Island — Tropical Minecraft-style survival. Beautiful island setting. Fishing, farming, ancient ruins.
  • Pixelmon Town — Pokemon + Minecraft mashup. Catch creatures AND build your world. The crossover you didn't know you needed.
  • Nova Craft — Merge-crafting with discovery mechanics. Different genre but same creative satisfaction of combining elements.
  • 99 Nights in the Forest — Minecraft meets horror survival. Beautiful cel-shaded world with genuine tension.
  • Block Blast — Not a sandbox, but captures the block-placement satisfaction in puzzle form.
