Why it fits a quiet break

2048 is a quiet number puzzle with a single, readable decision on every turn. It suits a light-focus break because there is no opponent and no need to memorise a complicated control scheme. A good stopping point is simply after creating one larger tile or when the board starts to feel busy.

How to play

Use the arrow keys or swipe to slide all tiles. Combine matching numbers.
