Top 12 IO Games Ranked — Best .io Browser Games 2024

By omggame.store editorial team · 5 min read

IO games defined a generation of browser gaming — simple, competitive, and endlessly replayable. After extensive playtesting, here's our definitive ranking of the 12 best IO games available right now.

  1. Snaker IO — The evolution of Snake. 50+ players on one map. Boost mechanic adds risk/reward depth. The most polished IO game.
  2. Basketball IO — Competitive 3D basketball against real players. Dribble, shoot, defend. Surprisingly deep.
  3. CobraZ IO — Fast-paced arena FPS. Quick matches, smooth performance. Pure run-and-gun fun.
  4. ArmedForces IO — Class-based military FPS. Sniper, medic, assault, engineer. Tactical depth.
  5. Drako IO — Fantasy MOBA in a browser. Choose heroes, level up in matches, fight in arenas.
  6. Skibidi Toilet IO — Viral arena battler. Push opponents out. Viral YouTube phenomenon.
  7. Snake Classic — The original IO game format. 2-player local mode.
  8. CS Online — Tactical FPS with economy management. The most competitive IO shooter.
  9. FPS Strike — Arena FPS with ranking system. Fast matches, competitive matchmaking.
  10. Fall Brainrots — Chaotic multiplayer parkour. Bizarre characters, wobbly physics, hilarious races.
  11. Crazy Cattle 3D — Wobbly cow battle royale. Headbutt farm animals off platforms.
  12. Steal Brainrots Multiplayer — Quirky creature defense strategy. Deploy brainrots against human opponents.
