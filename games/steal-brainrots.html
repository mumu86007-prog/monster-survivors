Why it is on the trend desk

Steal Brainrots Multiplayer is included on our Trend Desk because the brainrot-style multiplayer format is showing up strongly in current browser and social-game conversation. It is deliberately more competitive and noisier than the calm games in this collection: the point is a short, silly session of placing units and reacting to another player, not settling into a long relaxing routine.
